PRIVACY is the compliance surface for data collection, processing, retention, and user rights across all CANONIC products and services. One policy, all domains.

---

## Constraints

```
MUST:     Publish privacy policy at canonic.org/privacy/ (canonical URL)
MUST:     Enumerate every data type collected, its purpose, and retention period
MUST:     Enumerate every third-party processor (analytics, payments, hosting)
MUST:     Declare legal basis for processing (consent, legitimate interest, contract)
MUST:     Provide contact method for data subject requests (access, deletion, correction)
MUST:     Specify jurisdiction (Florida, USA) and governing law
MUST:     Cover all product domains (canonic.org, hadleylab.org, mammochat.com, caribchat.ai, gorunner.pro)
MUST:     Update policy when new data collection or processors are added
MUST:     Version the policy with effective date
MUST NOT: Collect data without declared purpose
MUST NOT: Share personal data with third parties beyond declared processors
MUST NOT: Retain data beyond declared retention period
MUST NOT: Require account creation for public content access
```
