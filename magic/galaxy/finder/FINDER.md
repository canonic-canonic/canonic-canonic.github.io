## Axiom

**FINDER is hierarchical navigation for the governed topology.** The galaxy graph is the truth; the Finder is the interface. Users navigate into scopes like folders, read intelligence like files, and act through conversation. The force-directed graph is a secondary view, toggled on demand. Breadcrumbs are the spine.

---

## Purpose

FINDER replaces spatial memory with hierarchical memory. In a force-directed graph, you must remember where nodes are positioned and pan/zoom to find them. In FINDER, you click into folders and the breadcrumb tells you where you are. Every click either navigates deeper or opens a detail surface. The full-width card grid shows children at the current level with compliance rings, COIN balances, and child counts. The same left/right drawer model from the graph view applies: left = INTEL scoped to the current breadcrumb level, right = DETAIL for the selected card.
