| Lineage | Founder | Philosophy | Key Practice |

## Vedanta Schools (Philosophical)

| School | Founder | Reality | God-Soul Relation | Path |
|--------|---------|---------|-------------------|------|
| Advaita | Shankara (8th c.) | Brahman alone real, world = maya | Identity | Jnana (knowledge) |
| Vishishtadvaita | Ramanuja (11th c.) | Brahman + souls + world all real | Part-whole | Bhakti (devotion) |
| Dvaita | Madhva (13th c.) | God, souls, world eternally distinct | Eternal difference | Bhakti + grace |
| Shuddhadvaita | Vallabha (16th c.) | Everything IS Brahman, nothing illusory | Non-difference | Grace (pushti) |
| Achintya Bheda-Abheda | Chaitanya (16th c.) | Inconceivable simultaneous oneness + difference | Both/neither | Chanting + love |
