The Prophet designated Ali as his successor at Ghadir Khumm. Leadership passes through the Ahl al-Bayt by divine appointment, not community consensus.

**Example**: "Of whomever I am the mawla, Ali is his mawla" (Hadith of Ghadir Khumm). This is THE claim that separates Shia from Sunni — succession is divine appointment, not shura (consultation). The dispute is not personal preference but theological principle.

### 2. Infallibility of the Imams (Isma)

The Imams are divinely protected from error in matters of faith and law.

**Example**: Twelve Imams from Ali (d. 661) to Muhammad al-Mahdi (occultation 874). Each Imam is ma'sum (sinless, infallible in religious guidance). This contrasts with Sunni caliphs, who are fallible administrators. The Imam IS the living proof of divine guidance.

---

### 3. Martyrdom of Husayn (Karbala)

The massacre at Karbala (680 CE) is the defining event — Husayn's sacrifice against tyranny is the moral center of Shia identity.

**Example**: Ashura — annual commemoration with mourning processions, passion plays (ta'ziyeh), and lamentation. Karbala IS the T — the event that defines Shia temporal consciousness.

---

### 4. Occultation of the Twelfth Imam (Ghayba)

The Twelfth Imam, Muhammad al-Mahdi, entered occultation in 874 CE. He will return as the Mahdi to establish justice before the Day of Judgment.

**Example**: Minor occultation (874-941) maintained contact through four deputies (sufara). Major occultation (941-present) means no direct representative. The Hidden Imam's absence creates the jurisprudential gap that the marja'iyya system fills — scholars govern in the Imam's absence.

---

### 5. Marja'iyya (Source of Emulation)

In the Imam's absence, the most learned mujtahid becomes the marja al-taqlid — the source laypeople follow for religious rulings.

**Example**: Grand Ayatollahs (Sistani in Najaf, Khamenei in Qom) serve as living authorities. Each Shia Muslim chooses a marja and follows his fatawa. This is NOT a pope — multiple marjas coexist. The system IS distributed religious authority within clerical hierarchy.

---

### 6. Taqiyya (Precautionary Dissimulation)

Concealing one's faith under persecution is religiously permitted. Survival preserves the community.

**Example**: Quran 16:106 — "Whoever disbelieves in Allah after belief, except one who is forced while his heart is at peace with faith..." Centuries of minority status under Sunni rule made taqiyya an operational survival mechanism. It is NOT deception but permitted self-preservation under duress.
