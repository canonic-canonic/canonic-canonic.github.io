## Mahayana (Great Vehicle)

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| CANON | Pali + Mahayana sutras (Prajnaparamita, Lotus, Avatamsaka) |

| Formula | ENTERPRISE |
| Regions | China, Korea, Japan, Vietnam, Taiwan |
