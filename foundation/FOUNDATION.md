## THREE AXIOMS

Three first principles keep the system simple enough to explain to a non-technical buyer and strict enough to enforce in code.

**TRIAD**
Every project declares its rules (CANON), vocabulary (VOCAB), and plain-language purpose (README). Like a passport: identity, language, intent.

**INHERITANCE**
Rules flow from top to bottom. A regulated industry sets constraints. Products built in that industry inherit them automatically. No loopholes.

**INTROSPECTION**
The system checks itself. Missing definitions and gaps in evidence get flagged before anyone has to argue about them.
