## Privacy Policy

**Effective date: April 1, 2026**

CANONIC Foundation ("we," "us," "our") operates the following products and services: canonic.org, hadleylab.org, mammochat.com, mammochat.ai, caribchat.ai, gorunner.pro, and related subdomains. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information across all of these properties.

### What We Collect

**Analytics data.** We use third-party analytics services to understand how visitors use our sites. These services collect standard internet log information and visitor behavior patterns through cookies and similar technologies. The analytics providers we use are: Google Analytics (GA4), LinkedIn Insight Tag, Meta Pixel, Twitter/X Pixel, and Reddit Pixel. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

**Chat session data.** When you interact with our AI chat products (MammoChat, CaribChat, OmicsChat), we collect your conversation inputs and the system's responses. For clinical products, this may include health-related information you voluntarily provide such as cancer staging, receptor status, and treatment history. Chat sessions are processed by our LLM providers (currently OpenAI and Anthropic) to generate responses.

**Account data.** If you create an account on any of our products, we collect your email address and display name. For GoRunner, we also collect payment information which is processed by Stripe.

**Calendar and contact data.** Our internal tools process Apple Calendar and Apple Contacts data for scheduling and contact resolution. This data is not transmitted to external services and remains on local infrastructure.

### How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect to operate and improve our products, generate analytics and usage reports, provide AI-powered responses in chat products, process payments, and communicate with you about your account. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

### Third-Party Processors

We share data with the following third-party processors solely for the purposes described above:

### Data Retention

Analytics data is retained according to each provider's default retention settings (typically 14 months for GA4). Chat session data is retained for the duration of the product's operation and for research purposes under IRB-approved protocols where applicable. Account data is retained until you request deletion. Payment data retention is governed by Stripe's policies and applicable financial regulations.

### Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at founder@canonic.org. We will respond to data subject requests within 30 days. You may also opt out of analytics tracking by using browser extensions that block tracking scripts, or by enabling Do Not Track in your browser settings.

### Clinical Data

MammoChat and CaribChat process health-related information under IRB-approved research protocols. Clinical trial participation is governed by separate informed consent documents. The MammoChat clinical trial is registered at [ClinicalTrials.gov (NCT07214883)](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07214883).

### Children

Our products are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
