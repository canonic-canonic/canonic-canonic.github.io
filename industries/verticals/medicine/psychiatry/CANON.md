## Axioms

### 1. Diagnostic Validity

All diagnoses MUST meet DSM-5 or ICD-11 criteria with documented symptom assessment.

```
Diagnosis requires:
- Symptom inventory
- Duration criteria met
- Functional impairment documented
- Rule-out conditions considered
- Differential diagnosis documented
```

### 2. Measurement-Based Care

Treatment MUST be guided by validated outcome measures administered at regular intervals.

```
Required measurements:
- Baseline assessment before treatment
- Progress monitoring (typically q2-4 weeks)
- Treatment response evaluation
- Remission/recovery criteria tracking
```

### 3. Safety Assessment

Suicide and violence risk MUST be assessed and documented at each encounter.

```
Safety assessment includes:
- Suicidal ideation (SI)
- Suicidal intent and plan
- Access to means
- Protective factors
- Risk stratification
- Safety planning
```

### 4. Privacy Enhancement

Mental health records MUST comply with enhanced privacy protections (42 CFR Part 2 for substance use).

```
Enhanced protections:
- Explicit consent for disclosure
- Segmented records capability
- Audit trail for access
- Minimum necessary principle
- Re-disclosure prohibition notice
```

### 5. Collaborative Care

Treatment MUST involve care coordination when multiple providers are involved.

```
Collaboration includes:
- Primary care integration
- Specialist referrals
- Care manager coordination
- Family involvement (with consent)
- Crisis services linkage
```
