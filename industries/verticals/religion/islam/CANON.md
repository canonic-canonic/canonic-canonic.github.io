## Axioms

### 1. Tawhid (Divine Unity)

There is no god but God. Tawhid is the absolute declaration from which all else follows.

**Example**: "La ilaha illallah, Muhammadur rasulullah" — the Shahada. Two clauses: (1) theological claim (monotheism), (2) prophetic claim (Muhammad as messenger). Declaring it with conviction IS conversion.

---

### 2. Quranic Authority

The Quran is the literal, unaltered word of God, revealed in Arabic to Muhammad over 23 years.

**Example**: "This is the Book about which there is no doubt" (Quran 2:2). Unlike Christian scripture (authored by humans, inspired by God), the Quran claims DIRECT divine authorship. The evidence is the text itself — inimitable (i'jaz).

---

### 3. Prophetic Seal

Muhammad is the final prophet in a chain from Adam through Abraham, Moses, Jesus. Revelation is complete.

**Example**: "Muhammad is not the father of any of your men, but the Messenger of God and the Seal of the Prophets" (Quran 33:40). This temporal claim closes the prophetic timeline — no new revelation after 632 CE.

---

### 4. Ummah (Community)

The global Muslim community transcends nationality, ethnicity, language. Membership by Shahada.

**Example**: Hajj — millions from every nation circle the Kaaba in identical garments. The pilgrimage IS the relational demonstration: one community, one direction (qibla), one God.

---

### 5. Sharia (Sacred Law)

Islamic law governs worship, commerce, family, criminal justice, international relations.

**Example**: The Five Pillars (Shahada, Salat, Zakat, Sawm, Hajj) are the operational minimum. Fiqh (jurisprudence) expands into comprehensive life governance through four Sunni schools (Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, Hanbali) and Ja'fari (Shia).

---

### 6. Institutional Form

Mosque, madrasa, waqf (endowment), ulama (scholarly class), and historical caliphate provide structure.

**Example**: The Friday khutbah (sermon) at the jami (congregational mosque) IS structural governance — weekly assembly, centralized address, communal prayer in formation (saf).
